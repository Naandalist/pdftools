Questions

Good to know.

Is PDF Tools really free?

Yes. All 24 tools and the scanner are free, with no limits on files or pages. The app shows a few ads to pay for its development.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool runs on your phone, and the files you make are saved in the app's own private storage. We don't have a server that could receive them.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Every tool, the scanner and text recognition work in airplane mode. The internet connection is only used to load ads.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 7.0 or newer. The built-in PDF viewer needs Android 9 or newer; on older phones PDFs open in your usual viewer instead.

Why does the app ask for camera and file access?

The camera is used only while you scan a document. File access lets the Files tab list the PDFs already on your phone; you turn it on yourself in system settings, and the app works without it.

Can I undo a redaction or a password?

Redaction removes content for good, which is the point. Every tool saves a new file and leaves your original as it is, so keep the original if you might need it.
