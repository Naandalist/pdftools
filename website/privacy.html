The short version

  • Every tool runs on your phone. Your documents are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • There is no account, no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To do that, Google collects some device data, including your advertising ID.
  • We don't sell personal data. We don't receive personal data from the ads, only totals such as how many ads were shown.

1. Who we are

PDF Tools is an Android app published by Naandalist ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes and what choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

2. Your documents

Every tool in PDF Tools, including the scanner, compression, OCR (text recognition) and the PDF viewer, runs entirely on your device.

  • The PDFs, images and scans you open or make are processed on your phone and are never sent to us. We have no server that could receive them.
  • Files you save are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Text recognition uses a model built into the app. Page images and the text read from them stay on the device, and it works without a connection.
  • When you share or export a file, it goes to the app you choose, and that app's own privacy policy applies.

3. Advertising

PDF Tools is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The ads are loaded by the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the SDK collects and sends to Google:

DataWhy it is used
Advertising IDAd delivery, frequency capping, measurement and, if allowed, personalised ads
IP address and approximate location derived from itChoosing ads suited to your region, fraud prevention
Device and app information (model, Android version, language, app version)Showing ads that work on your device, diagnostics
Ad interactions (ads shown, taps)Measurement, billing advertisers, fraud prevention

This data goes to Google, not to us. Google processes it under its own Privacy Policy. You can read how Google uses data from apps that show its ads at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown and the revenue they earned. These reports do not identify you.

Ads never see your documents, and no document content is used to choose ads.

4. What we don't collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number
  • No analytics or usage tracking added by us
  • No crash reporting
  • No contacts, messages or precise location

We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it with anyone except as described in this policy.

5. Permissions

The app asks for a permission only when a feature needs it, and never uses it in the background.

  • Camera: used only while you scan a document. Camera frames are processed on the device and are not stored unless you save a scan.
  • All files access: optional. It lets the Files tab list the PDFs already on your phone. You turn it on yourself in system settings, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • Photos and files you pick: when you choose an image or PDF with the system picker, the app reads only that item.
  • Internet: used to load ads. No document is sent over the internet.

6. Feedback

If you choose Send feedback in Settings, a Google Form opens in your browser. Whatever you write there, and an email address if you choose to give one, is sent to us through Google Forms. We use it only to reply and to improve the app. Google Forms is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

7. Keeping and deleting data

  • Your files stay on your phone until you delete them in the app (Settings has an option to delete all saved files) or uninstall the app.
  • We keep feedback and emails only as long as needed to deal with them.
  • Data collected by Google for ads is kept according to Google's retention policies.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: on most phones, open Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads). Deleting it stops personalised ads based on that ID.
  • Limit personalised ads: use the same Ads settings, or manage your choices at My Ad Center.
  • Stop all collection: uninstalling the app stops all data collection by the app and the ads SDK.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data. We hold no personal data about you beyond any feedback you send us; contact us to exercise your rights over that. For data collected for ads, you can also contact Google through its Privacy Policy page.

9. Children

PDF Tools is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Keeping documents on your device means there is no copy on a server to be breached. Saved files are stored in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. Your phone's own lock screen and encryption protect them too, so keep those turned on.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change matters to how your data is handled, the app will also tell you before you carry on.
